Prophetic Authority & Authoritative Decrees

Authoritative Commanding Prayer Based on Ezekiel 37:4–5

“Again he said unto me, Prophesy upon these bones, and say unto them, O ye dry bones, hear the word of the LORD. Thus saith the Lord GOD unto these bones; Behold, I will cause breath to enter into you, and ye shall live.”

— Ezekiel 37:4–5

Heavenly Father, in the supreme, all-powerful name of Jesus Christ,

I come before You acknowledging that You alone are God, and that all authority in heaven and on earth has been given unto Jesus Christ.

Standing firmly in my spiritual position in Christ Jesus, I declare and prophesy over every dry, dead, or stagnant area of my life according to Ezekiel 37:4–5.

From this position of divine authority, I enforce God's original blueprint across my life, my marriage, my health, my bloodline, and my finances with immediate effect.

STEP 1 — Deep Repentance Before Commanding Restoration

Father, before I execute any decree of authority, I humble myself completely under Your mighty hand. I ask You to search me and cleanse my conscience.

  • Expose every sin that requires repentance.
  • Expose every attitude that needs correction.
  • Expose every habit that must end.
  • Expose every relationship that needs boundaries.
  • Expose every decision that has taken me away from Your will.

I repent before You of pride, lust, sexual immorality, dishonesty, greed, hatred, unforgiveness, bitterness, jealousy, envy, anger, rebellion, disobedience, and every compromise I knowingly or unknowingly made.

Specific Repentance Concerning My Relationship with Teddy

Father, I specifically confess the fornication that occurred before marriage between me and Teddy Nettugu, my wife. We crossed a boundary You had established for sexual purity before marriage. We compromised what You commanded. I take responsibility for my part without blaming circumstances, emotions, pressure, or anyone else.

Through that relationship, our son Magezi Joshua was conceived. Father, I ask You to forgive me. Forgive Teddy. Have mercy upon us. I surrender that entire history to You.

Where we compromised Your integrity, restore us. Where we opened doors through sinful choices, teach us to close them through repentance, obedience, wisdom, and holiness.

And concerning our son Joshua, I reject shame and condemnation concerning his existence. He is precious and his life belongs to You. Let his future be governed by Your grace and purpose rather than by the mistakes surrounding his conception.

STEP 2 — Receiving God's Mercy

Father, I stand upon Your unshakeable Word:

  • Psalm 51: Wash me thoroughly from my iniquity and cleanse me from my sin.
  • 1 John 1:9: As I confess my sins, I trust Your promise of forgiveness and cleansing.
  • Isaiah 1:18: Cleanse what has been stained.
  • Isaiah 43:25: Blot out my transgressions.
  • Psalm 103:3: You are the God who forgives iniquity and heals disease.
  • 2 Chronicles 7:14–18: Teach me humility, prayer, repentance, and obedience.

Father, I approach You through the shed blood of Jesus Christ. Have mercy upon me, cleanse me, restore me, and establish me in Your righteousness.

From my position of authority in Jesus Christ, I stand upon Ezekiel 37:4–5 and speak directly to every dry bone in my life:

“THUS SAYS THE LORD GOD: BEHOLD, I CAUSE BREATH TO ENTER INTO YOU, AND YOU SHALL LIVE!”
