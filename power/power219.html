PHASE 01 — ENTER WITH WORSHIP, ADORATION AND THANKSGIVING

Psalm 100:3–4
“Know ye that the LORD he is God: it is he that hath made us, and not we ourselves; we are his people, and the sheep of his pasture. Enter into his gates with thanksgiving, and into his courts with praise.”
AUTHORITATIVE WORSHIP DECREE

Father, Jehovah God Almighty, I deliberately establish worship as the first movement of this declaration. I exalt You above money, career, business, relationships, reputation, possessions, opportunity and every earthly ambition. You are Creator, King, Sustainer, Judge and Father. I declare Your greatness over my life, my household and every legitimate responsibility entrusted to me.

I praise You for Your power displayed in creation, Your faithfulness to Abraham, Your deliverance of Israel from Egypt, Your preservation of Daniel, Your victory over Goliath, Your deliverance of Jehoshaphat, Your protection of David, and the supreme victory of Jesus Christ through His death and resurrection.

I worship You because no created power outranks You. I enthrone Your will above my preferences and establish Your glory as the governing purpose of my life.

✓ Decree: God is first. His Kingdom is first. His glory is first.

PHASE 02 — FORGIVENESS, HUMILITY AND DEEP REPENTANCE

1 John 1:9
“If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.”
AUTHORITATIVE REPENTANCE DECREE

I forgive those who have wronged me. I release vengeance to God and refuse to carry bitterness into my marriage, parenting, career, finances or future generations.

I confess my own sin specifically and honestly before God. I repent of pride, selfish ambition, disobedience, dishonesty, anger, impure thoughts, careless speech, unforgiveness, sexual immorality, financial irresponsibility and every action contrary to God's Word.

I specifically repent of the premarital sexual relationship between myself and Teddy Nettugu before our marriage, including the circumstances in which our son Magezi Joshua was conceived. I take responsibility for my own conduct without placing guilt or shame upon Joshua. Joshua is not condemned by the circumstances of his conception.

I repent for every failure to honor my marriage covenant, every failure of patience, communication, purity, love, responsibility and Christ-centered leadership. I establish my marriage under truth, faithfulness and mutual honor.

I also confess and renounce every sinful pattern I knowingly participated in. I receive forgiveness through Christ and establish a continuing commitment to holiness and obedience.

Psalm 51:10
“Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me.”
CLEAN-HEART DECREE

I decree a clean heart, a steadfast spirit, disciplined desires, truthful speech, pure motives and obedient conduct. I refuse to use spiritual language to excuse practical disobedience. My faith produces responsible action.

PHASE 03 — MY IDENTITY AND POSITION IN CHRIST

Colossians 3:3
“For ye are dead, and your life is hid with Christ in God.”
IDENTITY DECREE

I decree that my identity is not defined by ancestral failure, poverty, sickness, rejection, disappointment, missed opportunities or past limitations. My life is hidden with Christ in God. I belong to Jesus Christ.

Ephesians 2:6
“And hath raised us up together, and made us sit together in heavenly places in Christ Jesus.”
KINGDOM POSITION DECREE

I establish my position in Christ: I approach God with confidence because of Christ, not because of personal merit. I exercise responsibility, wisdom and faith from this identity. I reject fear-driven spirituality and establish Christ-centered authority, obedience and discernment.

Ephesians 1:3–4
“Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who hath blessed us with all spiritual blessings in heavenly places in Christ: According as he hath chosen us in him...”
PURPOSE DECREE

I decree that my life, marriage, parenting, career, technical gifts, business ideas, resources and future are submitted to God's purposes. I reject opportunities that require compromise. I choose what is true, righteous, lawful, useful and aligned with God's Word.

PHASE 04 — MATTHEW 6:33: KINGDOM FIRST

Matthew 6:33
“But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you.”
SUPREME PRIORITY DECREE

I decree that God's Kingdom is the first priority of my life. God is my King. Jesus Christ is my Lord. I submit my decisions, desires, plans, relationships, resources, work, technology, career, finances and future to His government.

I command my priorities into divine order. God first. Kingdom first. Righteousness first. Everything else occupies its proper place beneath that order.

I reject money as my master. Money is a servant and stewardship responsibility. I reject possessions as my identity. I reject success without righteousness. I reject wealth that requires disobedience. I reject opportunities that require compromise.

I decree that my pursuit of wealth never outranks my pursuit of God. I work diligently, steward faithfully, give generously, plan wisely, save responsibly, invest prudently and conduct business honestly.

PHASE 05 — KINGDOM RIGHTEOUSNESS IN EVERY AREA

Matthew 6:31–32
“Therefore take no thought, saying, What shall we eat? or, What shall we drink? or, Wherewithal shall we be clothed?... for your heavenly Father knoweth that ye have need of all these things.”
FREEDOM FROM ANXIETY DECREE

I command anxiety about necessities to lose its governing place in my thinking. I refuse panic-driven decisions. I establish disciplined planning, prayerful judgment, diligence and trust in God.

I decree order over my income, expenses, debts, savings, investments, giving and business capital. I identify financial leaks and correct them. I reject waste and establish stewardship.

Kingdom application: Trust in God does not cancel work, budgeting, professional development, legal compliance, medical care, contracts, due diligence or responsible financial management. Kingdom stewardship integrates faith with disciplined action.

PHASE 06 — WISDOM, KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING

Proverbs 3:13–18
“Happy is the man that findeth wisdom, and the man that getteth understanding... Length of days is in her right hand; and in her left hand riches and honour... She is a tree of life to them that lay hold upon her.”
WISDOM ACTIVATION DECREE

I decree wisdom into my decisions, architecture, engineering work, leadership, marriage, parenting, investments, contracts, negotiations, business formation and long-term planning. I command knowledge to become understanding and understanding to become wise action.

I decree discernment to distinguish profitable opportunity from distraction, truth from deception, sustainable wealth from temporary appearance, and righteous advancement from compromising advancement.

I decree that my software engineering and full-stack architectural skills are governed by wisdom, excellence, integrity and service. I establish a pattern of learning, building, documenting, testing, improving and delivering value.

Proverbs 24:3–4
“Through wisdom is an house builded; and by understanding it is established: And by knowledge shall the chambers be filled with all precious and pleasant riches.”
HOUSEHOLD AND ENTERPRISE DECREE

I decree that wisdom builds my household. Understanding establishes it. Knowledge fills it. I establish businesses, companies, systems, intellectual property, investments and productive assets through competence, discipline and lawful opportunity.

PHASE 07 — TRUTH, GRACE, POWER, GUIDANCE AND STRENGTH

John 8:31–32
“If ye continue in my word, then are ye my disciples indeed; And ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free.”
TRUTH DECREE

I decree truth into my thoughts, speech, marriage, finances, contracts, technology, business records, relationships and decisions. I reject deception, exaggeration, hidden manipulation and dishonest gain. Truth governs my household and enterprise.

Titus 2:11–12
“For the grace of God that bringeth salvation hath appeared to all men, Teaching us that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should live soberly, righteously, and godly...”
GRACE DECREE

I decree that God's grace trains me toward righteousness rather than excuses disobedience. I receive grace to repent, learn, endure, work, forgive, lead, serve and remain faithful.

2 Timothy 1:7
“For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.”
POWER AND SOUND-MIND DECREE

I decree power governed by love, courage governed by wisdom and a sound mind governing every decision. I reject fear-based paralysis and establish disciplined action.

PHASE 08 — FAMILY, BLOODLINE AND GENERATIONAL STEWARDSHIP

Psalm 112:1–3
“Blessed is the man that feareth the LORD, that delighteth greatly in his commandments. His seed shall be mighty upon earth... Wealth and riches shall be in his house...”
MULTI-GENERATIONAL KINGDOM STEWARDSHIP DECREE

I decree Kingdom order over the family line represented in this prayer: Amosi Lwomunwa, Gilsomu Waibi, Buyinza Patrick, Kisakye Paul, Teddy Nettugu, Magezi Joshua, and the descendants who come after us.

I do not declare inherited guilt upon descendants. I establish a new pattern of responsibility in Christ: truth, education, health stewardship, marriage faithfulness, financial discipline, entrepreneurship, generosity, service, lawful inheritance and wise asset management.

I decree that the family does not merely pursue paycheque-to-paycheque survival. We build skills, savings, productive assets, businesses, intellectual property, companies, land where appropriate, investments and systems that can serve future generations.

I decree that every generation is equipped to build upon what is good, correct what is harmful, and transfer wisdom rather than destructive patterns.

PHASE 09 — CAREER, BUSINESS, COMPANY AND OPPORTUNITY

Deuteronomy 8:18
“But thou shalt remember the LORD thy God: for it is he that giveth thee power to get wealth, that he may establish his covenant...”
WEALTH-CREATION CAPACITY DECREE

I decree capacity to create value and produce wealth through legitimate work. I command my professional skills to increase in depth, usefulness and market value. I establish excellence in software engineering, full-stack architecture, product development, consulting, leadership and entrepreneurship.

I decree strategic relationships, legitimate contracts, profitable projects, excellent delivery, repeatable systems, responsible investments and company establishment aligned with God's righteousness.

I reject failed endeavors caused by poor preparation. I command myself to prepare thoroughly, validate ideas, manage risk, document agreements, deliver excellence and learn from every result.

Psalm 90:17
“And let the beauty of the LORD our God be upon us: and establish thou the work of our hands upon us; yea, the work of our hands establish thou it.”
WORK-OF-HANDS DECREE

I decree establishment over the work of my hands. I command my work to become useful, excellent, sustainable and capable of serving people while producing lawful income and long-term value.

PHASE 10 — FINANCIAL ORDER, SAVING, INVESTMENT AND MULTI-GENERATIONAL WEALTH

Proverbs 21:5
“The thoughts of the diligent tend only to plenteousness; but of every one that is hasty only to want.”
FINANCIAL DISCIPLINE DECREE

I decree diligence over haste. I command my financial decisions to be planned, measured and documented. I reject impulsive spending, destructive debt, dishonest gain, financial secrecy and careless commitments.

I establish emergency reserves, purposeful saving, lawful investing, productive assets, business reinvestment, generous giving and long-term planning according to my actual resources and responsibilities.

Proverbs 13:22
“A good man leaveth an inheritance to his children's children...”
INHERITANCE DECREE

I decree that my financial vision extends beyond consumption. I establish assets, knowledge, character, education and productive systems that can bless children and grandchildren. I command myself to build something durable rather than merely appear successful.

PHASE 11 — MARRIAGE, CHILDREN AND HOUSEHOLD GOVERNMENT

Joshua 24:15
“...as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD.”
HOUSEHOLD DECREE

I decree that my household belongs to Jesus Christ. I command my marriage to operate through love, truth, communication, faithfulness, patience and mutual honor.

I decree that Teddy Nettugu is honored as my wife and co-heir in Christ. I decree that Magezi Joshua is raised without shame, with identity, wisdom, education, discipline, love and knowledge of God. I decree the same intentional foundation for every future child, grandchild and descendant.

I establish a household culture where children learn Scripture, education, financial literacy, work ethic, technical skills, integrity, generosity, emotional maturity and responsible stewardship.

PHASE 12 — PROTECTION AND PEACE

Psalm 91:1–2
“He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty. I will say of the LORD, He is my refuge and my fortress...”
PROTECTION DECREE

I declare God as my refuge and fortress. I command my decisions, movements, home, marriage, children, work and legitimate assets to be governed by wisdom and protected according to God's will.

I establish practical vigilance alongside faith: wise travel, secure homes, responsible financial controls, healthy boundaries, lawful contracts, professional diligence and timely medical attention where needed.

Isaiah 54:17
“No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper...”
NO-WEAPON DECREE

I decree that opposition does not determine my outcome. False accusations, deception, intimidation and destructive schemes do not govern my life. I respond with truth, wisdom, lawful action, prayer and perseverance.

PHASE 13 — RESTORATION AND REDEMPTION OF TIME

Joel 2:25
“And I will restore to you the years that the locust hath eaten...”
RESTORATION DECREE

I decree restoration of lost time through redeemed opportunity, disciplined action, learning, healing, reconciliation, renewed productivity and wise stewardship. I refuse to remain trapped by yesterday.

I command wasted lessons to become wisdom, closed doors to become direction, failures to become education, and recovered opportunities to be handled with maturity.

Ephesians 5:15–16
“See then that ye walk circumspectly... Redeeming the time, because the days are evil.”
TIME-REDEMPTION DECREE

I decree disciplined use of time. I command my calendar, attention, skills and energy toward God's purposes. I reject distraction that repeatedly consumes time without producing value.

PHASE 14 — THE KINGDOM-FIRST DAILY OPERATING ORDER

MORNING COMMAND OF PRIORITIES

Every morning I establish God first. I establish Scripture before distraction, prayer before panic, wisdom before impulse, work before excuses, stewardship before consumption, and service before selfish ambition.

I command my day into order: worship → Word → wisdom → priorities → diligent work → stewardship → family → service → review → gratitude.

FINANCIAL OPERATING COMMAND

I command every legitimate income stream to be tracked. I command expenses to remain visible. I command unnecessary leakage to be corrected. I command savings to be intentional. I command investments to be researched. I command business capital to be separated from personal consumption. I command contracts to be reviewed before commitment.

CAREER OPERATING COMMAND

I command continuous learning, excellent execution, professional integrity, strategic networking, documentation, portfolio development and responsible leadership. I refuse to wait passively for breakthrough while neglecting preparation.

PHASE 15 — KINGDOM-FIRST DECLARATION OVER THE FUTURE

Jeremiah 29:11
“For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, saith the LORD, thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you an expected end.”
FUTURE DECREE

I decree that my future is not governed by fear. I submit my plans to God's wisdom and establish the courage to act when the path is clear.

I decree meaningful career advancement, excellent work, legitimate business growth, useful companies, financial stability, family unity, healthy relationships, education, generosity and multi-generational stewardship insofar as these align with God's will.

I decree that every future generation connected to this household receives a better foundation: faith, truth, wisdom, education, discipline, health stewardship, financial literacy, productive assets, responsible inheritance and a commitment to serve God and people.

FINAL KINGDOM-FIRST VERDICT

I now establish the governing order of my life:

GOD FIRST.
KINGDOM FIRST.
RIGHTEOUSNESS FIRST.
TRUTH FIRST.
WISDOM FIRST.
FAITHFUL STEWARDSHIP FIRST.

I decree that money does not rule me. Fear does not rule me. Pressure does not rule me. Vanity does not rule me. Comparison does not rule me. Past failure does not rule me.

Jesus Christ is Lord over my identity, marriage, household, career, technical gifts, finances, businesses, companies, opportunities, resources and future.

I decree that my household will pursue blessing without abandoning righteousness, wealth without abandoning wisdom, advancement without abandoning integrity, and influence without abandoning service.

I decree that the work of my hands becomes useful. I decree that my mind becomes disciplined. I decree that my skills become excellent. I decree that my finances become orderly. I decree that my family becomes intentional. I decree that my resources become instruments of Kingdom purpose.

I do not chase “the addition” while neglecting the Kingdom. I seek the Kingdom first, and I trust God with the addition while I faithfully do the work assigned to me.

My highest pursuit is God Himself: to know Him, walk with Him, obey Him, represent Him and bring glory to Him.

According to Matthew 6:33, I establish this order over my life today and recommit to it every day.

KINGDOM FIRST • RIGHTEOUSNESS FIRST • GOD FIRST
In Jesus Christ's mighty name. Amen.
