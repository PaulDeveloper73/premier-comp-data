4. The Shield of Faith (Ephesians 6:16)

Father, I lift the shield of faith. Every fiery dart against my mind, confidence, family, and calling is extinguished. I reject fear, doubt, discouragement, and hopelessness.

5. The Helmet of Salvation (Ephesians 6:17)

Father, protect my mind. My thoughts, imagination, and plans belong to Christ. I bring every thought into captivity to the obedience of Christ.

6. The Sword of the Spirit (Ephesians 6:17)

Father, Your Word is my weapon. When challenges come, I answer with Scripture. When fear comes, I answer with faith. When temptation comes, I answer with truth.
