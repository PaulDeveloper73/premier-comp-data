Seated With Christ: Software Engineering Career Consecration

Part 25 — Declaration Over Spiritual Discipline, Staying Close to Jesus in the End Times, Overcoming Worldliness, Protecting My Faith While Building Career, Wealth & Technology

Keeping My Lamp Burning Until the Return of Christ
Operating from Ephesians 2:6 Authority

My Foundation: Jesus is My First and Final Purpose

"And raised us up together, and made us sit together in heavenly places in Christ Jesus."
— Ephesians 2:6

Father, in the mighty name of Jesus Christ:

I declare:

  • I am seated with Christ.
  • My greatest achievement is knowing Christ.
  • My greatest success is fulfilling God's will.
  • My greatest treasure is eternal life with Jesus.

I declare I will not allow:

  • Career to replace Christ.
  • Money to replace Christ.
  • Technology to replace Christ.
  • Recognition to replace Christ.
  • Success to replace Christ.
"For what profit is it to a man if he gains the whole world, and loses his own soul?"
— Matthew 16:26

Father, guard my soul. Keep my heart faithful. Keep my eyes fixed on Jesus.

Declaration: Jesus Remains My Foundation

"For no other foundation can anyone lay than that which is laid, which is Jesus Christ."
— 1 Corinthians 3:11

Father: Build everything upon Christ—my marriage, family, career, business, technology, finances, and future.

I decree: Everything I build must stand on the foundation of Jesus.

Declaration for the End Times

"Watch therefore, for you do not know what hour your Lord is coming."
— Matthew 24:42

Father, awaken my spirit. Help me remain watchful, prepared, and faithful.

I declare: I will not be distracted by the spirit of this age or consumed by temporary things. I will live with eternity in mind.

Declaration: My Lamp Will Remain Burning

"Then the kingdom of heaven shall be likened to ten virgins..."
— Matthew 25:1-13

Father: Give me oil for my lamp, spiritual hunger, a love for Your Word, and a desire for Your presence.

I declare: My faith will not grow cold, my prayer life will not die, and my passion for Jesus will not disappear.

"Hold fast what you have, that no one may take your crown."
— Revelation 3:11

I hold firmly to Christ.

Declaration Over Daily Prayer Life

"Men always ought to pray and not lose heart."
— Luke 18:1

Father: Build consistency in me. Teach me to pray when life is easy and when life is difficult. Teach me to seek You daily.

I decree: Prayer is my strength, my connection, my weapon, and my lifestyle.

Declaration Over My Love for God's Word

"Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path."
— Psalm 119:105

Father: Make Your Word my guide. Teach me Scripture, and give me understanding and revelation.

I declare: The Bible shapes my decisions, character, and future.

Declaration Against Worldly Distractions

Father, I take authority over every distraction that competes with Your purpose. I reject excessive entertainment, wasteful habits, uncontrolled desires, pride, comparison, and materialism.

"Do not love the world or the things in the world..."
— 1 John 2:15

Father: Set my heart on eternal things.

I decree:

  • I use technology; technology does not control me.
  • I use money; money does not control me.
  • I pursue success; success does not control me.

Declaration Over Technology and Digital Discipline

"Whoever has no rule over his own spirit is like a city broken down, without walls."
— Proverbs 25:28

Father, give me wisdom in this digital generation. Help me control my screen time, attention, online habits, and digital influence.

I declare: I have self-control, discipline, and wisdom.

Declaration Against Temptation

"Therefore submit to God. Resist the devil and he will flee from you."
— James 4:7

Father: I submit to You, resist temptation, and walk in holiness.

I decree: Sin will not dominate my life, my flesh will not control me, and my desires are submitted to Christ.

Declaration Over My Career Priorities

"In all your ways acknowledge Him, and He shall direct your paths."
— Proverbs 3:6

Father, as I pursue software engineering excellence, keep my priorities aligned.

I declare: God first, family second, purpose-driven work third. Everything else follows Your order. Lord, direct my career, ambitions, and plans.

Declaration Against Pride in Success

"Pride goes before destruction, and a haughty spirit before a fall."
— Proverbs 16:18

Father: Keep me humble. When I succeed, keep me grateful; when I receive recognition, keep me dependent on You.

I declare: I will never forget the God who gave me wisdom.

Declaration Over My Family's Faith

"But as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD."
— Joshua 24:15

Father: Let my family know You, let my children love You, and let my home honor You.

I declare: My household, home, and future generations belong to Jesus.

Declaration of Endurance

"Let us run with endurance the race that is set before us, looking unto Jesus..."
— Hebrews 12:1-2

Father: Help me finish my race, remain faithful, endure challenges, and keep my eyes on Jesus.

I declare: I will finish strong. I will not abandon my faith, my calling, or God's purpose.

Final End-Times Kingdom Engineer Declaration

Today I declare:

  • I am seated with Christ. Jesus is my Lord, foundation, purpose, and destination.
  • I build technology, but I worship God.
  • I pursue excellence, but I pursue Christ more.
  • I seek success, but I seek the Kingdom first.
  • I develop skills, but I depend on the Holy Spirit.
  • My faith remains strong, my lamp remains burning, my heart remains faithful, my family remains covered, my career remains surrendered, and my purpose remains established.

Until Jesus returns: I will watch, pray, serve, build, love, and remain faithful.

In the mighty name of Jesus Christ. Amen.
