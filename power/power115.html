Prophetic Authority & Intercession

Commanding Prayer Based on Ezekiel 37:4–5

“Prophesy upon these bones, and say unto them, O ye dry bones, hear the word of the LORD.”

— Ezekiel 37:4

Heavenly Father, in the mighty name of Jesus Christ,

I come before You acknowledging that You alone are God and that all authority belongs to You.

As I pray through the prophetic picture of Ezekiel 37, I do not place my confidence in the words themselves, but in You—the living God who gives life, restores what is broken, and brings hope where circumstances appear hopeless.

I ask You to breathe life into every area of my life that has become dry, broken, stagnant, or unproductive.

STEP 1 — Deep Repentance Before Commanding Restoration

Father, before I speak any declaration of restoration, I humble myself before You. I ask You to search me completely.

  • Expose every sin that requires repentance.
  • Expose every attitude that needs correction.
  • Expose every habit that must end.
  • Expose every relationship that needs boundaries.
  • Expose every decision that has taken me away from Your will.

I repent before You of pride, lust, sexual immorality, dishonesty, greed, hatred, unforgiveness, bitterness, jealousy, envy, anger, rebellion, disobedience, and every compromise I knowingly or unknowingly made.

Specific Repentance Concerning My Relationship with Teddy

Father, I specifically confess the fornication that occurred before marriage between me and Teddy Nettugu, my wife. We crossed a boundary You had established for sexual purity before marriage. We compromised what You commanded. I take responsibility for my part without blaming circumstances, emotions, pressure, or anyone else.

Through that relationship, our son Magezi Joshua was conceived. Father, I ask You to forgive me. Forgive Teddy. Have mercy upon us. I surrender that entire history to You.

Where we compromised Your integrity, restore us. Where we opened doors through sinful choices, teach us to close them through repentance, obedience, wisdom, and holiness.

And concerning our son Joshua, I reject shame and condemnation concerning his existence. He is precious and his life belongs to You. Let his future be governed by Your grace and purpose rather than by the mistakes surrounding his conception.

STEP 2 — Receiving God's Mercy

Father, I stand upon Your Word:

  • Psalm 51: Wash me thoroughly from my iniquity and cleanse me from my sin.
  • 1 John 1:9: As I confess my sins, I trust Your promise of forgiveness and cleansing.
  • Isaiah 1:18: Cleanse what has been stained.
  • Isaiah 43:25: Blot out my transgressions.
  • Psalm 103:3: You are the God who forgives iniquity and heals disease.
  • 2 Chronicles 7:14–18: Teach me humility, prayer, repentance, and obedience.

Father, I do not approach You claiming personal righteousness. I approach You through Jesus Christ. Have mercy upon me. Cleanse me. Restore me. Correct me. Teach me. Strengthen me.

STEP 3 — Repentance Concerning My Bloodline

Father, I bring my family history before You. You know every generation, ancestor, secret, wound, act of righteousness, and act of sin. I ask You to expose and cleanse every destructive pattern that has continued through my family.

I repent of every form of:

Idolatry & Occult Practice
Sexual Immorality & Adultery
Witchcraft, Divination & Sorcery
Bloodshed, Murder & Violence
Hatred, Revenge & Unforgiveness
Pride, Rebellion & Greed
Theft, Fraud & Deception
Addiction & Drunkenness
Financial Irresponsibility
Marital Destruction & Oppression

And every other work of the flesh described in Galatians 5:19–21.

Father, where destructive patterns have existed, let them stop with me. Do not allow me to reproduce destructive behavior. Teach me to establish a new legacy of righteousness.

STEP 4 — Repentance for the Territory Where I Live

Father, I also pray concerning the territory in which I live. I do not claim guilt for sins I did not personally commit, but I ask You to have mercy upon the community around me.

Where there has been bloodshed, violence, sexual immorality, corruption, exploitation, occultism, injustice, abuse, hatred, or oppression, bring mercy and righteousness.

Heal the wounded. Protect children. Restore families. Expose corruption. Bring justice where justice is required. Let me personally contribute righteousness rather than destruction to the place where I live.

STEP 5 — Now, Lord, Speak Life Into the Dry Places

Father, as Ezekiel was commanded to prophesy to the dry bones, I now bring every dry and broken area of my life before You.

Hear the Word of the Lord.

To Spiritually Dry Areas: Receive the life, wisdom, renewal, and strength that God desires to give.
To Marriage: Receive restoration, communication, forgiveness, faithfulness, love, patience, purity, and unity.
To Emotional Health: Receive healing, peace, stability, courage, and renewed hope.
To Career & Skills: Receive wisdom, creativity, excellence, legitimate opportunity, discipline, and productive growth.
To Financial Areas: Receive wisdom, order, diligence, legitimate opportunities, responsible stewardship, provision, and sustainable growth.
To Spiritual Walk: Receive renewed hunger for God, prayer, Scripture, holiness, worship, wisdom, and obedience.

STEP 6 — Commanding Health & Rejecting Fear of Disease

Father, I now place my health, Teddy's health, Joshua's health, and the health of my future descendants before You. I reject fear, fatalism, and the belief that destructive family patterns must automatically control every generation.

I pray for healing and protection concerning HIV/AIDS, sexually transmitted infections, cancer, tumors, high blood pressure, heart disease, stroke, ulcers, severe anemia, sickle-cell disease, blood disorders, diabetes, kidney disease, liver disease, respiratory diseases, tuberculosis, malaria, neurological disorders, autoimmune diseases, gastrointestinal diseases, reproductive diseases, fertility problems, chronic pain, and all hereditary or unknown conditions.

Direct Declarations:

  • To every disease and infection affecting my body: You do not have permission to define my identity.
  • To every harmful infection: I reject fear and ask God for healing, proper diagnosis, competent treatment, and restoration.
  • To every cancerous growth or abnormality: I place you before the authority of God and ask Him to destroy what is harmful and restore what is healthy.
  • To ulcers and gastrointestinal disorders: I ask God for healing, wisdom, proper treatment, and restoration.
  • To abnormal blood conditions & cardiovascular issues: I ask God to restore health, give medical professionals wisdom, and grant discipline for healthy living.

STEP 7 — Commanding Financial Restoration

Now I address the dry places in my finances and career:

  • Destructive financial patterns: Be exposed.
  • Resource-wasting habits: Be corrected.
  • Financial irresponsibility: End through wisdom.
  • Fear preventing action: Lose your influence.
  • Legitimate opportunities: Come into alignment.
  • Value-creating skills: Be developed with excellence.

Father, teach me how to earn, save, invest, give, manage, build, and steward resources responsibly.

STEP 8 — Renunciation of False Spiritual Allegiances

Jesus Christ alone is my Lord.

I renounce every spiritual practice contrary to the Word of God: occultism, witchcraft, divination, sorcery, spiritism, and false worship.

I renounce every vow, oath, dedication, covenant, alliance, or agreement that contradicts my allegiance to Jesus Christ. I specifically renounce every alleged spiritual association described in my Christian understanding as Jezebel, queen of heaven, marine spirits, water spirits, or any other power contrary to God.

I declare: I do not belong to them. I do not worship them. I do not serve them. I do not seek their guidance. I do not submit my future to them. I belong to Jesus Christ.

STEP 9 — Prayer Concerning Name, Identity, and Descendants

Father, concerning every place where my name may have been associated with anything contrary to Your will, I surrender that matter to You.

I surrender my name, identity, body, marriage, children, future descendants, career, finances, future, reputation, purpose, calling, and entire family line to Jesus Christ.

And concerning the language of my DNA, Father, I surrender my biological inheritance completely to You. Let no fear concerning genetics control me. Where there are legitimate hereditary health risks, give us wisdom, screening, prevention, treatment, and appropriate medical care.

STEP 10 — A Declaration Over Future Generations

Father, I pray for my wife, my children, my grandchildren, my great-grandchildren, my great-great-grandchildren, and every generation that may come after me:

  • Inherit a better spiritual & practical foundation
  • Inherit wisdom rather than confusion
  • Inherit faith rather than fear
  • Inherit integrity rather than deception
  • Inherit discipline rather than destruction
  • Inherit healthy marriages & financial wisdom

STEP 11 — The Three Impenetrable Walls

WALL ONE

The Blood of Jesus

Let the finished work of Jesus Christ be the foundation of our protection over my life, Teddy, Joshua, our household, marriage, and future generations.

WALL TWO

The Holy Spirit

Holy Spirit, fill our household. Guide us, correct us, strengthen us, and give us discernment, wisdom, and self-control.

WALL THREE

The Word of God

Let Scripture govern our decisions, relationships, marriage, parenting, finances, and future as an unshakeable foundation.

STEP 12 — Final Commanding Declarations

In the name of Jesus Christ, I declare that my life, marriage, wife, child, future children, descendants, career, finances, and future belong to God.

Every destructive cycle must end. Every destructive habit must be confronted. Every pattern of sin must be rejected. Every pattern of financial irresponsibility must be corrected.

EVERY DRY AREA: LIVE!

Hear the Word of the Lord. Rise from the place of despair into obedience, wisdom, diligence, faith, hope, righteousness, and the purposes God has ordained.

STEP 13 — Sealing the Prayer

Heavenly Father, I seal this prayer by placing everything into Your hands. I do not trust in formulas or human words; I trust in You and in Jesus Christ. Give me wisdom to act, courage to repent, discipline to change, humility to learn, and strength to persevere.

In the Mighty Name of Jesus Christ, I Pray.
Amen.
