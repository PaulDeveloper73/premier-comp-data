“As the hart panteth after the water brooks, so panteth my soul after thee, O God. My soul thirsteth for God, for the living God: when shall I come and appear before God?”

— Psalm 42:1–2

PART I — STANDING IN SEATED AUTHORITY & ADORATION OF THE GODHEAD

Heavenly Father, Almighty God, Creator of heaven and earth, I come before You in the supreme, holy, and exalted name of Jesus Christ.

I take my stand from the spiritual location You assigned me through the finished work of the Cross:

  • Ephesians 2:6 — I am raised up together with Christ and seated with Him in heavenly places, far above all principalities, powers, rulers of darkness, and spiritual wickedness in high places.
  • Colossians 3:1–4 — My life is hid with Christ in God. I set my affection on things above, where Christ sits on the right hand of God.

From this throne-room realm, I bow my heart to praise, adore, and magnify You, the eternal Godhead—Father, Son, and Holy Spirit!

Holy, Holy, Holy is the Lord God Almighty! The heavens declare Your glory, the earth is full of Your goodness, and Your kingdom endures throughout all generations. I adore You for Your infinite wisdom, majesty, righteousness, and unsearchable love!

PART II — AN UNQUENCHABLE HUNGER FOR GOD & THE HOLY SPIRIT

Father, I am not coming merely to seek Your hand for material gifts or temporary answers. I AM CRYING OUT FOR YOU! I want You—Your heart, Your nature, Your holiness, Your voice, and Your living fellowship.

Thirst for the Godhead (Psalm 63:1 & Psalm 27:4): O God, You are my God; early will I seek You! My soul thirsts for You in a dry and thirsty land. One thing have I desired of the Lord, that will I seek after: to dwell in the house of the Lord all the days of my life, to behold the beauty of the Lord!
Deep Intimacy with the Holy Spirit (John 14:16–17 & John 16:13): Holy Spirit, You are the Spirit of Truth, my Comforter, Teacher, and Guide. I cry out for an unbroken, intimate relationship with You. Teach me to hear Your still, small voice through the Scriptures. Preserve me from grieving You (Ephesians 4:30). Make me completely Spirit-led and Word-governed in all things!

I Remind You of the Hunger of Your Ancient Servants:

Moses (Exodus 33:13–18): “If I have found grace in Your sight, show me now Your way... Please, show me Your glory!” Give me Moses' refusal to advance without Your physical presence!
Elijah & Elisha (2 Kings 2:9): Ignite in me the double-portion hunger that calls down divine fire, destroys idol worship, and transforms national atmospheres!
Solomon (1 Kings 3:9 / 8:10–11): Give me an understanding heart to discern good from evil, and let Your glory cloud so fill my home that no dark power can stand!
Samson (Judges 16:28): Strengthen me by Your Spirit to fulfill my original divine blueprint without falling prey to compromise or spiritual blindness!
