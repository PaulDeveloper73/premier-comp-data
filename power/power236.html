Spirit-Filled Identity & Divine Assignment

FINDING GOD'S PURPOSE & THE DEEP CRY FOR HIS WILL

"For in Him we live and move and have our being... Created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand that we should walk in them."

Colossians 3:3 Ephesians 2:6, 2:10 2 Chronicles 7:14-18 Matthew 6:33

I. The Core Position & Foundation

Hidden in Christ

Colossians 3:3

Your real life and divine purpose are hidden with Christ in God. You cannot find your identity in worldly work alone; it is safely anchored in Him.

Seated in Heavenly Places

Ephesians 2:6

Raised up and seated with Him in heavenly realms. You operate from victory and authority, not from baseline survival or blind repetition.

Covenant & Repentance

2 Chronicles 7:14-18

Humbling oneself, praying, seeking His face, and turning from self-reliance unlocks divine healing and established purpose for you and your house.

II. Breaking the Blind Cycle: From Survival to Assignment

You expressed a deep holy ache: "I am tired of living blindly, just eat, sleep, go to work. I want to know the real who I am, what is the exact purpose God has for me, what to do step by step to fulfill it, then I can die a happy man after fulfilling God's will."

"Our hearts are restless until they rest in You." — St. Augustine

That dissatisfaction with mundane repetition is not a defect; it is God-placed holy hunger calling you into your true spiritual design.

III. Step-by-Step Practical Blueprint (Spirit-Filled Discipleship)

Step 1

Surrender & Die to Self First

Galatians 2:20 • Luke 9:23

Before receiving your divine assignment, lay down your personal ego, human timelines, and self-made plans. Yielding control opens space for Christ to live through you.

Step 2

Be Freshly Filled with the Holy Spirit

Ephesians 5:18 • Acts 1:4-8

Being born again grants salvation; being continually Spirit-filled grants power and discernment for your call. Pray daily for an ongoing fresh infilling.

Step 3

Renew Your Mind to Perceive His Will

Romans 12:2 • Daniel 1

Transformation happens when your mind is saturated with Scripture. Discernment is not mystical guessing; it is the natural capacity of a mind trained in truth.

Step 4

Learn to Hear & Test His Voice

John 10:27 • 1 Samuel 3 • Colossians 3:15

The Spirit speaks through Scripture, inner peace, circumstances, and wise counsel. Always test subjective impressions against the written Word and mature believers.

Step 5

Identify Spiritual Gifts & Quiet Competence

1 Corinthians 12 • 1 Samuel 17:34-36

Like David tending sheep in obscurity, examine what skills, burdens, and spiritual gifts God has already placed in your hands. Unseen faithfulness prepares you for public calling.

Step 6

Where Design Meets Human Need

Nehemiah 1:3-4

Purpose sits at the intersection of your God-given ability, what breaks your heart in the world, and where doors open for real kingdom service.

Step 7

Obey the Next Step (Headlight Faith)

Hebrews 11:8 • Luke 16:10

Abraham left home without seeing the entire map. Do not wait for a 30-year blueprint; execute obedience on the immediate instruction before you.

Step 8

Plant in Spirit-Filled Community

Acts 13:1-3 • Proverbs 15:22

Paul and Barnabas were commissioned out of a praying local body. Divine calling is confirmed, protected, and unleashed in community, not isolation.

IV. Scriptural Proof-Points: How Purpose Unfolded

Paul (Saul of Tarsus) — Acts 9

Interrupted on the Damascus road. His old zeal and self-righteous identity died before his true apostle identity was revealed.

The Disciples — Acts 1-2

They had 3 years of teachings, yet Jesus instructed them to wait for Spirit empowerment before stepping into global purpose.

David — 1 Samuel 17

Fought lions and bears in private obscurity. His faithfulness in small shepherd tasks qualified him to face Goliath and lead Israel.

Nehemiah — Nehemiah 1-2

A cupbearer whose specific grief over ruined walls combined with administrative capability to rebuild a nation's defense.

Audited & Standardized Prayer Blueprint

A DEEP CRY FOR GOD HIMSELF

A Prayer of Hunger, Surrender, Identity, Purpose, Kingdom, Righteousness, Bloodline & Divine Alignment

Father, in the mighty name of Jesus Christ,

I come before You with a hunger that words cannot fully explain.

Father, I thirst for You. As the deer pants for streams of water, so my soul pants for You. My soul is thirsty for the living God.

I do not merely want Your blessings; I want You. I do not merely want answers; I want Your presence. I do not merely want to know what You can give me; I want to know who You are.

I do not merely want a successful life; I want a life that has been completely surrendered to Your will.

Father, I am crying out for deeper intimacy with You. Take me beyond shallow Christianity, beyond routine prayer, beyond simply waking up, eating, working, sleeping, and repeating the same cycle.

I don't want to live blindly. I don't want to arrive at the end of my life having accumulated things but never discovering why You created me.

Father, reveal Yourself to me.

Reveal the depths of Your heart to me. Teach me Your ways, Your voice, Your timing, Your thoughts, Your priorities, and Your Kingdom. Give me a heart that genuinely longs for You. Let my greatest hunger become God Himself.

FATHER, I WANT TO KNOW YOU

Father, I want to know You—not merely know about You. Take me deeper into fellowship with You.

Holy Spirit, teach me how to commune with the Father. Teach me how to pray when I have no words. Teach me how to remain in Your presence when there is no emotional feeling. Teach me how to seek You when I don't understand what You are doing.

Teach me how to wait for You, how to hear You, and how to distinguish Your voice from my own desires, fears, ambitions, emotions, and assumptions.

Father, make me sensitive to You. Let my spirit become attentive to Your Spirit. When You whisper, teach me to recognize Your voice. When You correct me, give me humility to receive correction. When You tell me to wait, give me patience. When You tell me to move, give me courage. When You tell me to stop, give me obedience.

When You close a door, don't let me force it open. When You open a door, don't let fear keep me outside.

Father, I want Your will more than I want my own way.

LORD, SHOW ME WHO I AM IN YOU

Father, before I existed in the sight of men, You knew me. Before I entered my mother's womb, You knew me. Before anyone knew my name, You knew my name. Before I had accomplishments or a career, You knew me.

Before I understood myself, You understood me completely. You know the person You designed me to be, the purpose for which You formed me, the gifts You placed inside me, the assignments You prepared for me, the places You intend for me to serve, and the people I am supposed to influence.

You know the doors I am supposed to enter and those I am never supposed to enter. You know the seasons of my life—the beginning, the middle, and the end.

Father, therefore I come to the One who knows me completely.

Tell me who I am in You. Strip away every false identity—received from people, built from achievements, failure, rejection, comparison, or fear. Strip away every identity that does not come from You. Father, let me see myself through Your eyes.

LORD, I CRAVE TO KNOW WHY YOU CREATED ME

Father, I don't want to miss my assignment. I don't want to spend years walking in circles, spending my strength on things You never asked me to build, chasing opportunities You never ordained, or becoming successful at something that was never Your purpose for me.

I want Your purpose.

Not my imagined purpose, nor society's expectations, comparison, competition, pride, money, fame, or recognition—Your purpose.

Father, what were You thinking when You designed me? What did You place inside me? What burden, gifts, assignments, and Kingdom responsibilities have You entrusted to me?

What problem did You create me to help solve? What people did You intend me to serve? What must I build, protect, teach, serve, surrender, stop, start, learn, and become?

Father, reveal these things to me according to Your will and Your timing.

LORD, I SEEK FIRST YOUR KINGDOM

Father, Your Word says to seek first Your Kingdom and Your righteousness. Therefore, before I ask You for houses, money, opportunities, success, recognition, or possessions—I ask You for Your Kingdom.

Rule in me. Rule over my thoughts, desires, ambitions, decisions, relationships, marriage, children, work, finances, time, gifts, and future. Let Your Kingdom become the governing reality of my life.

Father, make righteousness more valuable to me than success, obedience more valuable than applause, holiness more valuable than popularity, Your presence more valuable than possessions, and knowing You more valuable than being known by people.

LORD, GIVE ME YOUR POWER FOR YOUR PURPOSE

Holy Spirit, I do not want power for myself; I want power to obey God, overcome sin, love, serve, endure, and stand when temptation comes. I want power to remain faithful when nobody is watching.

I want spiritual discernment, wisdom, understanding, courage, self-control, spiritual sensitivity, boldness to witness for Jesus, and the fruit of the Spirit visible in my life.

Father, empower me for whatever You have assigned me to do. Do not allow me to run ahead of Your grace or attempt Your assignment through human strength alone.

Holy Spirit, empower me for the purpose of God.

FATHER, TEACH ME YOUR TIMETABLE & NUMBER MY DAYS

Teach me Your timing.

I don't want merely to know what You want; I want to know when. When should I move, wait, speak, remain silent, start, stop, accept, refuse, build, rest, pursue, or release?

Father, teach me Your seasons. Help me number my days wisely. Don't let me waste years pursuing something out of impatience, miss an appointed season through fear, mistake delay for rejection, mistake opportunity for divine assignment, mistake emotion for Your voice, or mistake human pressure for Your will.

Align my clock with Your clock.

Align my calendar with Your calendar, my decisions with Your wisdom, and my movement with Your Spirit. From this day forward, teach me to walk according to Your timetable.

FATHER, REALIGN MY ENTIRE LIFE

Lord, if anything in me is out of alignment, reveal it. If my thinking is wrong, renew it. If my motives are wrong, purify them. If my priorities are wrong, reorder them. If my relationships, habits, desires, or ambitions are wrong, transform and crucify them.

If I am pursuing something You never commanded, give me grace to let it go. If I have become distracted, call me back. If I have become spiritually cold, awaken me. If I have become comfortable, disturb me again with hunger for You.

Father, realign me completely.

I surrender my past, present, future, plans, expectations, dreams, fears, ambitions, and timetable. Have Your way in me.

LORD, MY MARRIAGE & WIFE

Father, I place my marriage and my wife before You. I ask You to become the center of our union. Teach me how to love my wife according to Your will, lead with humility, serve, listen, communicate, forgive, and protect the unity of our home.

Remove selfishness, pride, unnecessary conflict, bitterness, misunderstanding, and every influence seeking to destroy what You ordained. Let our home honor Your presence. Reveal Your purpose for our marriage, showing us what to build and serve together to glorify Jesus.

LORD, MY CHILDREN

Father, I place my children before You. They belong to You. Guide me as their parent with wisdom to raise them according to Your will, without imposing my unfinished dreams upon them.

Help me recognize what You placed inside each child. Protect their minds, hearts, friendships, and future. Give them godly wisdom, hunger for Jesus, and personal knowledge of You. May they not merely inherit my name, but inherit a genuine relationship with God.

LORD, MY BLOODLINE & GENERATIONAL DESTINY

Father, I bring my entire bloodline before You. Work deeply within my family line. Where there has been brokenness, bring healing. Where there has been ignorance, bring truth. Where there has been destruction, bring restoration. Where cycles contradicted Your will, teach us a new way.

Raise a generation in my family that knows You—men and women who fear God, marriages that honor Christ, children who know Scripture, servants, leaders, intercessors, and faithful bearers of the Gospel.

Father, let whatever You desire to accomplish through my family begin with me. Make me faithful with my generation.

LORD, MY HOUSEHOLD, WORK, & TERRITORY

Father, I surrender my household, where I live, where I stay, where I work, and the territory surrounding me. Establish Your peace and righteousness in my sphere. Give me wisdom concerning how to conduct myself in my community and workplace.

Show me where to serve, pray, speak, or remain silent. Let my presence bring good, my work reflect Christ, my words reflect Christ, and my home reflect Christ. Make me a faithful steward of the territory You have entrusted to me.

FATHER, I SURRENDER THE NEED TO KNOW EVERYTHING

Lord, I surrender my demand to understand everything immediately. If You show me one step, I will take it. If You show me the next door, I will walk toward it. If You tell me to wait, I will wait.

I don't need the entire map if I have the Guide—You are the Guide. Lead me, correct me, stop me, redirect me, open doors, close doors, send people, remove people, give opportunities, and take away distractions. Whatever brings me into alignment with Your will, do it with mercy and wisdom.

FATHER, LET MY LIFE BECOME AN ANSWER

I want my life to be a testimony of what God did through a surrendered man. Let my life, marriage, children, work, decisions, and old age say: God was faithful.

When my earthly journey finishes, let me know I did not live for myself, but walked with You, loved You, obeyed You, and served Your purposes in my generation.

MY PERSONAL DEDICATION & COVENANT

So Father, here I am: Kisake Paul

I place myself before You—my identity, body, mind, spirit, gifts, weaknesses, past, present, future, wife, children, household, family, bloodline, work, resources, relationships, plans, dreams, ambitions, time, and days. I place everything before You.

TAKE ME DEEPER INTO YOU

Give me such hunger for Your presence that distance is unbearable. Give me love for Your Word, sensitivity to the Holy Spirit, hatred for sin, passion for Your Kingdom, humility to be corrected, courage to obey, wisdom to discern, patience to wait, and absolute surrender to say: "Not my will, Father, but Yours be done."

In the mighty, precious, powerful, and glorious name of Jesus Christ,

AMEN.
