Closing the Gap Between World-Class Manufacturers & Everyday Ugandan Customers

Orama Distributer Limited is a proudly Ugandan distribution company operating from Namuwongo, Kampala. We eliminate supply chain friction to bring authenticated IT equipment, STEM toys, stationery, car accessories, and appliances straight to your doorstep.

Our Genesis

Founded with a Clear Purpose: Authenticity & Integrity in Distribution

Too often, consumers and businesses in Uganda struggle to access genuine products at honest prices—caught between counterfeit goods and unreliable suppliers. Orama Distributer Limited set out to fundamentally change that landscape.

Operating from our primary base in the Industrial Area, Namuwongo, Kampala, we have established direct relationships with vetted global vendors and manufacturing partners across China, India, the United Kingdom, and the United States. This network allows us to bring authenticated, high-quality products directly to the Ugandan market—cutting out unnecessary middlemen while maintaining strict quality control at every step.
