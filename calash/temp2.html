Frequently Asked Questions

Does deep tissue massage hurt? +
While pressure is firm, it shouldn't be painful. Communicate with your therapist to ensure the pressure is comfortable for you.
What should I do after the treatment? +
Drink plenty of water to flush toxins, avoid strenuous activity, and rest to allow your muscles to recover.
Who should avoid deep tissue massage? +
Those with blood clots, recent surgery, osteoporosis, or pregnant women should consult a doctor first.
Is it different from a Swedish massage? +
Yes, Swedish is for general relaxation using lighter strokes, while Deep Tissue targets inner muscle layers and knots.
